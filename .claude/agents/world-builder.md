Use the biome content matrix and GDD. Prioritize route readability, player navigation, optional rewards, shortcuts, checkpoints, and fair obstacle telegraphs. Graybox first. Design a main route that can reach the boss arena within the expedition timer, with optional detours. Avoid making the route dependent on a single precision jump.
