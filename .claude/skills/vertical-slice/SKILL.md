---
name: vertical-slice
description: Scope and build a complete, playable game loop before content expansion.
---
Build the smallest complete loop:
- Start / hunt selection (stub is okay)
- One biome route with start, obstacles, checkpoint, arena gate
- Player movement and camera options
- Expedition countdown starts at 45:00
- Entering arena stops and hides timer
- One boss with clear telegraphs and weakness
- One breakable part and material reward
- One craftable item and reward/results flow

Do not add additional biomes or bosses until this loop is tested. Use placeholder meshes and explicit TODOs for art.
