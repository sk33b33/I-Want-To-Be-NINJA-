# Content Matrix

## Biomes
| Biome | Signature obstacles | Environmental hazards | Mood |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verdant Canopy | vines, roots, climb walls, canopy jumps | falling branches, hidden pits | lush, sunlit, humid |
| Emberwild | lava gaps, timed stones, vents | heat zones, ash, collapses | fiery, volcanic |
| Moonfen | mud, submerged routes, floating roots | poison water, currents, low visibility | eerie, glowing |
| Stormcrag | cliff climbing, ledges, waterfalls | wind gusts, rockfall, lightning | high-altitude, stormy |

## Element matrix (initial tuning only)
| Element | Strong against | Weak against | Terrain interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fire | Nature | Water | burns vines, lights braziers |
| Water | Fire | Lightning | cools heat, changes shallow water |
| Lightning | Water | Earth | energizes conductors |
| Earth | Lightning | Nature | breaks brittle rock, stabilizes |
| Nature | Earth | Fire | roots/entangles |

Avoid simplistic universal rules: each boss can have its own exceptions and status resistances.

## First-slice scope
- Biome: Verdant Canopy
- Route: one main path + one optional shortcut
- Traversal: run/jump/climb wall/gap
- Boss: Thornback Stag concept
- Part: antler
- Reward: antler material
- Craft: one weapon upgrade or armor piece
- Timer: 45:00; hidden/stopped at arena entry
